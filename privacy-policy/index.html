Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Pasa el prompt (@pasaelprompt) is published by The Usual Human. The Usual Human Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of these accounts to publish their own videos, carousels and stories to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and to answer comments on them. It is not offered to the public.

Data about the owner's accounts

When the owner authorises it, the tool receives access tokens that let it publish to the owner's own accounts, read those accounts' name and ID, read the comments and messages they receive, and reply to them. The tokens are kept only on the owner's computer.

Data about people who comment or write to us

When someone comments a keyword on one of our Instagram posts to receive a free guide, the tool replies to the comment publicly and sends that person one direct message. Guides are sent to people who follow the account: if the person answers that message, the tool checks whether they follow the account, and then sends the guide or a short reminder. To do this, and to avoid writing to anyone twice, it keeps a record with:

  • the comment's ID and text, the post it was made on, and when it was made;
  • the commenter's Instagram username and the IDs Instagram gives the tool for that person;
  • whether they follow the account at the time of checking (yes or no);
  • the status of each message (sent, answered, guide sent, failed, expired) and its date.

The tool looks at the account's recent messages only to see whether a person who commented the keyword has answered. It ignores every other message, does not store the content of any message, and never writes to anyone who has not commented the keyword or written to the account first.

This record is kept only on the owner's computer and deleted automatically 90 days after the last activity on each comment. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising, and nothing is sent to any server other than Instagram's own.

Your choices

You can ignore the message, delete the conversation or block the account at any time. To have your record deleted before the 90 days, write to the address below with your Instagram username.

YouTube

YouTube features use the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the security settings of their Google, TikTok or Meta account. Deleting the stored tokens and the record removes all data the tool keeps.
