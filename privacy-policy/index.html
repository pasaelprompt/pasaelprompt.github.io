Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Pasa el prompt (@pasaelprompt) is published by The Usual Human. The Usual Human Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of these accounts to publish their own videos, carousels and stories to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and to answer comments on them. It is not offered to the public.

Data about the owner's accounts

When the owner authorises it, the tool receives access tokens that let it publish to the owner's own accounts, read those accounts' name and ID, read the comments and messages they receive, and reply to them. The tokens are kept on the owner's computer. The one token that lets the tool answer comments and messages is also stored in the private settings of the hosting service that runs the tool's receiver (see below).

Data about people who comment or write to us

When someone comments a keyword on one of our Instagram posts to receive a free guide, Instagram notifies the tool of that comment. The tool replies to the comment publicly and sends that person one direct message with a button. Guides are sent to people who follow the account: when the person taps the button, the tool asks Instagram whether they follow the account, and then sends the guide as a link or a short reminder with a button to check again. Each tap gets one answer. To do this the tool receives from Instagram:

  • the comment's ID and text, the post it was made on, and when it was made;
  • the commenter's Instagram username and the ID Instagram gives the tool for that person;
  • when a button is tapped, that person's ID and which button it was;
  • whether they follow the account at the moment of checking (yes or no).

The tool does not build a list of the people who comment and does not store the text of any comment or message. It never writes to anyone who has not commented the keyword or tapped one of its buttons. When any other message reaches the account, it notes only that a message arrived and from which ID, and does nothing else.

The part of the tool that receives these notices runs on a hosting service (Vercel Inc.), not on the owner's computer. Each notice leaves a line in that service's run-time log with the comment's ID, the person's ID and username, which guide was asked for and whether the message was sent. The hosting service keeps that log for a short time (currently about one hour) and then deletes it. Records made on the owner's computer by an earlier version of the tool are deleted 90 days after the last activity on each comment. None of this is sold, shared or used for advertising, and nothing is sent anywhere other than Instagram and that hosting service.

Your choices

You can ignore the message, delete the conversation or block the account at any time. To ask what the tool holds about you, or to have it deleted, write to the address below with your Instagram username.

YouTube

YouTube features use the YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from the security settings of their Google, TikTok or Meta account. Deleting the stored tokens and the record removes all data the tool keeps.
